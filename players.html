Player EPA

Who produced each team's passing, rushing and receiving results — an accounting of what happened, with the league as the yardstick.

These numbers attribute team results to the players involved — each role's players sum to the team's figure on the EPA Summary page. They describe what happened, not how good anyone is: EPA credits the whole play to one player, so line play, scheme and opponent are all baked in.

Passing counts dropbacks, so sacks and scrambles are included. Receiving counts targets, not catches — throwaways and batted balls have no intended receiver and charge the passer alone, which makes receiving read slightly generous. A completed pass credits its full EPA to both the passer and the receiver, so never add the two roles together. Roles describe what a player did, not his position.
